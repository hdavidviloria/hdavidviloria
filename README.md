## About me

I'm primarily a business person who likes building.

I like getting my hands dirty and solving problems. A lot of how I work comes down to resourceful problem-solving: understanding what's actually going wrong, figuring things out as I go, and finding practical ways to make things more efficient.

I spend a lot of time thinking about difficult problems, especially those that affect how people work. Much of my previous work has focused on improving business processes across accounting, business intelligence, and marketing.

More recently, I've been taking that same problem-solving approach into product building: moving from problems inside individual businesses toward problems shared by larger groups of users.

## Selected applied work

### Computer Vision: Skyrise Realty

Parking visibility was poor, but installing dedicated counting hardware across multiple properties would have been expensive. I had very little prior machine-learning experience, so I taught myself enough computer vision to build a camera-based vehicle-counting system using the CCTV infrastructure already in place.

The result was a working proof of concept across four sites that avoided roughly **₱400,000 in hardware capex** and exposed discrepancies significant enough to justify further investigation.

**[Read the case study →](https://hdavidviloria.com/writing/cctv-revenue-assurance)**

<br>

### Finance Automation: ContactPoint360

One recurring accounts-payable workflow took roughly 30 minutes each time and depended heavily on manual processing. I mapped the process, found the repetitive decision points, and built an automation that reduced the workflow to roughly **15 seconds**.

I later applied the same approach to other finance and workforce-management tasks, including automating processing for **2,000+ BIR 2316 records** and building SQL / Power BI reporting workflows.

**[Read the case study →](https://hdavidviloria.com/writing/tax-process-in-house)**

<br>

### Business Systems: Oro China Group

I worked on a loyalty and analytics system designed around the constraints of a multi-branch retail business, including existing SAP Business One data, staff workflows, in-store hardware, QR and barcode interactions, and customer communications.

The interesting part wasn't just building the software. It was making the system fit the business that already existed.

<br>

### Marketing Automation: Golden Peak Hotel & Suites

I built customer-facing automation connecting web forms, messaging, and internal routing to reduce repetitive manual handling of enquiries.

This included a Facebook Messenger chatbot and automated routing through messaging APIs.
